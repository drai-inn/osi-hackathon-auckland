# ADR-0006 — Run 19–20 October, two days ahead of the global hackathon

**Status:** accepted · **Date:** 2026-09-19 · **Deciders:** Nick Jones
**Resolves:** [#12](https://github.com/drai-inn/drugs-surrogate-pipeline/issues/12), open question A7

## Context

The global [OSI Hackathon](../00-event/the-global-event.md) runs **21–22 October 2026**, and
Auckland is already listed as a 2026 site in planning. The obvious move was to align with the
official dates, which would also have made Auckland the first hub in the world to start.

Local reality says otherwise. **We cannot run past the 21st**, and the 21st itself collides with
other commitments. We need two contiguous days, and the two that are actually free are the 19th
and 20th.

## Decision

**Run Monday 19 – Tuesday 20 October.** Two days.

**Extending into Wednesday 21 October is an option we keep, not a plan we make.** If the work is
live and people want to keep going, the 21st is available and is — not coincidentally — the global
hackathon's opening day. That makes the extension a bridge rather than an overrun.

We therefore run as a **precursor to the global event rather than a concurrent hub**, and we frame
it that way deliberately instead of apologising for it.

### What the timing actually gives us

| | NZDT | UTC | EDT |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Our day 1 opens | Mon 19 Oct 09:00 | Sun 18 Oct 20:00 | Sun 18 Oct 16:00 |
| **Our day 2 closes** | **Tue 20 Oct 17:00** | **Tue 20 Oct 04:00** | **Tue 20 Oct 00:00** |
| Earliest global hub opens (NZ/Sydney local) | Wed 21 Oct 09:00 | Tue 20 Oct 20:00 | Tue 20 Oct 16:00 |
| US east-coast hubs open | Thu 22 Oct 02:00 | Wed 21 Oct 13:00 | Wed 21 Oct 09:00 |

**We finish 16 hours before the earliest hub in the world starts, and 33 hours before the US
hubs.** Every output we produce is on the table before anyone else begins.

That is a genuinely distinctive position, and arguably a more useful one than being hub number
thirteen: we are not competing for attention during the event, we are **supplying it**. The
line to use is *"two days ahead of the world"*, not *"first hub to start"* — which is no longer
true and must be removed everywhere it appears.

## Alternatives considered

| Option | Why not |
| --- | --- |
| Move to the official 21–22 Oct | We cannot run past the 21st, and the 21st has competing agendas. Not available |
| Run 20–21 Oct | Buys one day of overlap at the cost of the 21st's conflicts, and still isn't the official window |
| Split — one day now, one day on the 21st | Destroys the continuity that makes day 2's integrated run possible |
| Skip the global event entirely | Throws away the Slack, the mentors, the audience and the write-up route for no gain |

## Consequences

**Good.** The dates are certain, which unblocks invitations, the poster and the micro-repo — all of
which were waiting on this. The precursor framing is coherent and honest. Our outputs land in the
global event's opening rather than competing with 15 other hubs for attention mid-event. And the
optional third day becomes a clean on-ramp into the global hackathon for anyone who wants to
continue.

**Bad.** We lose the "first hub to start" line, which was the strongest hook in the collateral and
is now in eight files that need editing. We are probably not eligible for the prizes, and our
route into the write-up paper needs confirming rather than assuming — **ask, do not assume**
([A10](../02-scope/open-questions.md)). We will not have 1000+ people live in Slack while we work,
which costs us the mentor pool during the event itself.

**Mitigations.** Ask the organisers explicitly what a precursor hub can still participate in:
submitting projects, the write-up, the showcase. Publish our outputs *before* the global event
opens so they are usable by other hubs. Encourage anyone who wants to continue on the 21st to
register for the global event and carry the work in.

**Revisit if:** the organisers confirm that a precursor cannot submit or be credited *and* the
competing local commitments on the 21st evaporate. Neither is likely; both would have to change.
