## The four lines that should appear everywhere

0. **"Start small. Search hard. Scale only what survives."** The lead, and it goes first. We're
   testing whether a deliberately small experiment can predict what a large one would do. The drug
   selectivity work is the testbed, not the purpose — **don't bury this under the chemistry.**
1. **"We go two days ahead of the world."** Auckland runs 19–20 Oct and closes sixteen hours
   before the earliest hub in the global hackathon opens. Specific, checkable, and it explains
   why we exist rather than just when.
2. **"You will personally own a result the group uses."** Not "you will learn about AI for
   science", which is what everyone else's invitation says.
3. **Lead with the discipline's own question.** A statistician wants to know when a small
   experiment predicts a large one. A structural biologist wants to know if the poses are nonsense.
   Nobody outside computational chemistry wants to attend a drug-discovery hackathon.
