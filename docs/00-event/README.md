## The four lines that should appear everywhere

1. **"How far can a chain of surrogates get us?"** The lead, and it goes first. Every expensive step
   handed to a learned surrogate, nobody seems to have run the whole chain, and we want the number.
   Drug selectivity is the testbed, not the purpose, so don't bury the lead under the chemistry.
2. **"It might not be good enough yet. We'd still like the number."** This is a baseline we'll come
   back to. Every step is improving quickly and at some point the chain crosses a threshold.
3. **"You'll own a result the group uses."** Not "you'll learn about AI for science", which is what
   everyone else's invitation says.
4. **Lead with the discipline's own question.** A structural biologist wants to know if the poses
   are nonsense. Nobody outside computational chemistry wants to attend a drug-discovery hackathon.

And a posture note: **we're a local site.** Point people at the global event and its registration,
which is open to anyone. Don't make anything of our dates.
