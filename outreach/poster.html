How far can a chain of surrogates take us in biomolecular interactions?

Biology has a ladder of scales and machine learning now has one alongside it, most of it open-weight. Two days finding out how far up you have to climb before the answer can be trusted.

Systems · Proteins · Ligand binding · Drug discovery · Pharmacology

Who it's for

Bring your team, your expertise, and a problem you want to look at, or take one of our default targets. Chemists, physicists, structural biologists, statisticians, systems biologists, pharmacologists, clinicians, bioinformaticians, RSEs, and anyone new to it.

You don't need to be able to code. The agent writes the code and you bring the judgement about whether the answer is any good. If you can only make the opening, come for the opening and leave your team to it.
