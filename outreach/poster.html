OSI Hackathon

The Open Scientific Intelligence Hackathon, 4th edition — 120 projects across 16 sites in 2025, and a community paper each year crediting every team. One free registration covers both: Auckland 19–20 October and the global days 21–22 October. luma.com/ku88xh92

Four to start from. Bring your problem and take a space, or propose a theme of your own — the wider event takes agents, datasets, benchmarks, models and software too.

Who it's for

Bring your team, your expertise, and a problem you want to look at, or take one of our default targets. Chemists, physicists, structural biologists, statisticians, systems biologists, pharmacologists, clinicians, bioinformaticians, RSEs, and anyone new to it.

You don't need to be able to code. The agent writes the code and you bring the judgement about whether the answer is any good.
